About Me

I am a physicist with research experience in computational and theoretical approaches to biological and physical systems. My doctoral research at the School of Computational and Integrative Sciences, Jawaharlal Nehru University, focused on physics-informed modelling of chromosome dynamics and 3D genome organisation.

My work combines polymer physics, Langevin molecular dynamics, experimentally informed structural constraints and high-performance computing to investigate genomic organisation across local and global scales.
